## What was checked

### Discovery cohort (GSE103322)

Table 1 was reproduced in full from `WBP5_Table1_results_v2.csv`, itself
regenerated by `WBP5_Table1_recalc.ipynb`: group sizes (2,215 / 3,687), means
(2.3813 / 1.3231), positivity (68.89% / 28.97%), fold change (1.7998),
Mann–Whitney *p* (1.3268 × 10⁻¹⁰⁵), and Cliff's δ (+0.3075). The
primary-versus-lymph-node contrast matched at the same precision.

Table 2 was reproduced from `WBP5_Phase1_recalc.ipynb` (all eight Spearman
coefficients) and `WBP5_Table2_meanPCT_v2.csv` (group means and positivity for
the four p-EMT markers, plus the 1,526 / 689 split).

### Exploratory secondary cohort (GSE198315)

Every quantity in Section 3.1.2 was matched against
`gse198315_region_stats_summary.json` and five accompanying TSV exports:
cell counts, expression thresholds, high-expression group sizes, regional
composition, Wilson confidence intervals, ordered-region odds ratios,
primary-versus-nodal Fisher tests, patient-level Wilcoxon results, and
compartment enrichment folds.

The two chi-square statistics quoted in Section 3.1.2 are not stored in any
export, so they were recomputed from the underlying counts in
`gse198315_composition.tsv` (see `verify_recompute.py`). Both reproduce
exactly, including the number of cells with expected counts below five:

| Test | Recomputed | Manuscript |
|---|---|---|
| Region composition | χ² = 26.79, df = 6, *p* = 1.58 × 10⁻⁴, 1 sparse cell | χ² = 26.8, df = 6, *p* = 1.6 × 10⁻⁴, 1 sparse cell |
| Compartment composition | χ² = 692.5, df = 8, *p* = 2.99 × 10⁻¹⁴⁴, 2 sparse cells | χ² = 692.5, df = 8, *p* = 3.0 × 10⁻¹⁴⁴, 2 sparse cells |

Depth-corrected and uncorrected correlations were taken from
`gse198315_recalc/headline.tsv`, which stores both conditions side by side.
The patient-mean coefficients quoted in Section 3.2 come from
`correlations_full.tsv`; the WBP5–ITGA5 value in lymph-node proxy epithelium
(ρ = 0.890909, *p* = 5.42 × 10⁻⁴) matches the manuscript, and the KRT14 and
KRT17 patient-mean coefficients are non-significant as stated.

**Note on scale.** `correlations_full.tsv` contains both the `as_deposited`
and `CPM1e4_log1p` scales. Only the latter is used in the manuscript. Reading
the former by mistake yields, for example, ρ = 0.8667 rather than 0.891 for
the WBP5–ITGA5 lymph-node patient-mean correlation.

## Structure, docking poses, and chemistry

The pocket geometry in Section 3.4 and Table 3 was established by parsing the
DoGSiteScorer output (job `0f462a25`) together with the AlphaFold model.
P0 comprises twelve discontinuous residues between 38 and 58, with Drug Score
0.837, volume 229.62 cubic angstrom, surface 811.54 square angstrom, depth
24.90 angstrom, and a mean pLDDT of 79.4 computed from the B-factor column
over those residues. Eight pockets were detected in total. The docking grid
centre (-23.835, -0.451, 23.910) coincides with the P0 residue centroid, and
the volume centroid of `P_0_gpsAll.ccp4` confirms that the mesh drawn in
Fig. 6A is P0 and not another site.

The contact analysis in Section 4.2 was computed from the AlphaFold model and
the six seed-42 output poses, using heavy atoms only. Five residues (Ala41,
Lys42, Thr44, Phe45, Arg48) fall within 4 angstrom of the ligand in all six
poses. The Phe45 figures quoted in the manuscript (16-23 atom pairs at
3.22-3.58 angstrom) are the values recomputed after the Candidate_1 structure
was corrected; an earlier draft carried 16-29 pairs at 2.58-3.58 angstrom,
which derived from the superseded pyrrole pose.

Ligand identity for all ten compounds was cross-checked by InChIKey across the
docking scripts, the SwissADME export, and the ProTox 3.0 submissions.

## Library screen

The MTiOpenScreen figures in Section 3.6 come from job `V06821248773098`
(13 August 2026). An earlier job, `P12689576379061`, was run against the P_3
grid and is superseded; the manuscript cites the corrected job. The screen
returned 1,500 entries (1,037 unique drugs) spanning -8.7 to -5.7 kcal/mol
with a mean of -6.17 +/- 0.38. Pazopanib scored -5.8; 1,180 entries scored
better and 257 tied, placing it between ranks 1,181 and 1,437 of the 7,173
Drugs-lib entries, that is the top 16.5-20.0%. The remaining 79.1% of the
library falls below the -5.7 truncation and is unobserved.

## YAP/TAZ co-expression

The coefficients in Section 3.3 were computed from the full Puram 2017 matrix
(`HNSCC_all_data.txt`) over 2,215 malignant cells: WBP5 against a 22-gene
YAP/TAZ target signature (rho = +0.172), against YAP1 transcript
(rho = +0.019, not significant), and after adjustment for the p-EMT score
(partial rho = +0.064). The permutation null over 1,000 random 22-gene sets
had mean 0.041 and 97.5th percentile 0.120, giving an empirical p of 0.005.

The YAP/TAZ-to-p-EMT correlation of 0.56 quoted in the same paragraph is not
stored separately, but it is recoverable from the other three coefficients:
inverting the partial-correlation formula with rho(WBP5,YAP) = 0.172,
rho(WBP5,p-EMT) = 0.214 and partial rho = 0.064 gives 0.562, so the four
values are mutually consistent.

## Reproducing this audit

```bash
python verify_recompute.py
```

Requires `numpy` and `scipy` only. The script prints an OK/FAIL line for every
derived quantity and exits with a summary. It is self-contained: the input
counts and coefficients are embedded as literals, each annotated with the
source file it was read from, so the script serves as both a test and a record
of provenance.
