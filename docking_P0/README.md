# Docking at the P₀ pocket — note on Candidate_1

`docking_replicates_P0_summary.csv` gives the AutoDock Vina binding energies for the
ten docked ligands at the P₀ pocket, as the mean of five replicate runs with fixed
random seeds (42, 1042, 2042, 3042, 4042).

## Candidate_1 was re-docked after a structure correction

The Candidate_1 SMILES string originally recorded in `all_candidates.smi`
(`...Nc2ccnc2...`) could not be kekulized and was therefore invalid. It was first
restored as the pyrrole form (MW 335.43), but the molecular properties reported by
REINVENT4 for this compound (MW 347, TPSA 88.16) correspond to the pyridine form
(MW 347.44, TPSA 88.16). The pyridine form is therefore the molecule the generative
model produced, and Candidate_1 was re-docked in that form.

- Corrected (pyridine form), used throughout the manuscript and Supplementary
  Information: **−5.44 kcal/mol** (SD 0.055). Replicate-level output:
  `candidate1_pyridine_P0.csv`; regenerated by `redock_candidate1_pyridine.py`.
- Superseded (pyrrole form): −5.34 kcal/mol (SD 0.089). Retained for provenance in
  `../deprecated/`.

The summary table in this folder carries the corrected value. The other nine ligands
were unaffected by the correction and were not re-docked; their values are identical
in every version of the table.

The same corrected values appear in `../reproducibility/docking_replicates_P0_summary_v2.csv`.
